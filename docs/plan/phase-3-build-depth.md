# Phase 3: Build Depth (M3) — outline

**Goal:** the passive tree and the middle of the campaign, levels 6–15. This
page is an outline, refined by Phase 2's filing bead.

[Back to the plan](README.md)

**Earliest start:** the Phase 2 gate.

## Scope

- **The passive tree** (Appendix F): the curated sub-tree from
  `docs/data/passive-subtree.json`, allocation, respec, the screen
  (F §7), and its feel (H §9.3).
- **Bog** (levels 6–10) and **Desert ruins** (levels 11–15) (Appendix D
  §6.1): the Ranged, Shieldbearer, Armoured heavy, Runner, Flier, Splitter,
  Shaman, Burrower and Brute archetypes, and the Shallow Water, Shrine,
  Burning Ground and Grave tiles.
- **The Rust King** (D §7.2) and **Lachlann of Endless Lament** (D §7.3).
- **Positional and global auras, curses and marks** (Appendix B §2.6–§2.7)
  as their heroes become droppable.
- **Remaining combat mechanics** as their first users arrive: bleed,
  poison, electrocute, exposure, culling and leech.
- **Hero currencies:** Gemcutter's Prism, Realignment and Transference.
- **Music** for Bog, Desert ruins and the boss loop.

## Exit gate (draft)

- Levels 1–15 are playable end to end.
- The tree changes the board visibly.
- The headless simulator's level-length and kills-per-minute targets hold
  through level 15.
- Every milestone save loads.
