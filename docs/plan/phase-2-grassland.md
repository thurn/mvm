# Phase 2: Grassland (M2) — outline

**Goal:** the whole Grassland biome, levels 1–5, with the loops that make
loot matter between rounds: crafting, vendors, gamble and the loot filter.
This page is an outline. Bead 1.42 refines it into a full page from what M1
taught, the Phase 1 retrospective, and open `playtest` beads
([workflow § Filing a phase](workflow.md#filing-a-phase)).

[Back to the plan](README.md)

**Earliest start:** the M1 playthrough bead (1.39).

## Scope

- **Levels 2–5** (Appendix D §6.1): 5 lanes; the Armoured light, Swarm and
  Leaper archetypes; and Rock terrain, with map generation per D §8.2.
- **The Bloated Miller** (D §7.1): the 2-lane walker, telegraphs, the
  heavy-stun bar and the guaranteed unique. Appendix H §7.5 boss
  presentation and §3.9 loot fountains.
- **Strongboxes** (D §9) and H §3.8.
- **Crafting** (Appendix C §C.5): the item orb ladder that drops by area 6,
  Jeweller's Orbs, and salvage into shards. The H §9.2 reveals.
- **The Armorer, Recruiter and gamble tab** (GDD §11, E §5), and H §9.3.
- **The loot filter** presets (C §C.7, H §3.7).
- **The campaign map** (GDD §15) for levels 1–5, with first-clear reward
  previews.
- **The first-clear rewards** for levels 2–5 (E §4.5). Passive points accrue
  to be spent in Phase 3.
- **Data:** extend the importer's ID lists for the new archetypes, bases and
  mods up to area level 8, uniques, and every hero and spell that can drop
  by level 5.

## Likely parallel lanes

These are independent after the filing bead, keeping the graph wide:

- archetypes (one bead per pair), terrain and map generation;
- the boss sim, then boss presentation;
- Strongbox sim, then Strongbox presentation;
- the crafting rules (one bead per orb family), then each reveal;
- vendors and gamble, then their screens;
- the loot filter, and the campaign map;
- a calibration bead extending 1.37 to levels 1–5.

## Exit gate (draft)

- Levels 1–5 are playable from a new account to the Bloated Miller's defeat,
  with every M2 system juiced.
- The Appendix E §7 time-to-fun beats up to minute 30 hold in the headless
  simulator.
- The M1 and M2 milestone saves load.
- The Phase 2 mason pass and retrospective are done, and Phase 3 is filed.
